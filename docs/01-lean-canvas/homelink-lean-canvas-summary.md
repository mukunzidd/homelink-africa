# HomeLink: Lean Canvas (Summary)

*One-page snapshot for the team. Detailed version accompanies this document.*

**Product:** HomeLink, concierge-verified accommodation, cars, and tours for people coming to Rwanda.
**Date:** 2026-07-20 · **Stage:** Pre-build brainstorm · **Status:** Draft for team review

---

### ① Problem
1. Booking accommodation in Rwanda from abroad is **low-trust and fragmented**. Diaspora rely on WhatsApp, relatives, and informal brokers; you can't verify a place sight-unseen.
2. **Arrival is disjointed.** Stay, car, and getting around are separate, unreliable arrangements; nothing is guaranteed to be ready when you land.
3. The **local rental market is informal**, with opaque pricing, broker gatekeeping, and no quality or contract standard.

*Existing alternatives:* Airbnb / Booking (thin, unverified Rwanda inventory), Facebook / WhatsApp groups, personal networks, freelance brokers.

### ② Customer Segments
- **Earlyvangelists:** returning diaspora Rwandans booking a medium-term stay plus a car from abroad.
- Also: VISIT Rwanda tourists (short-term); everyday Rwandans (long-term homes and cars) on the core.

### ③ Unique Value Proposition
> **"Land in Rwanda with everything ready: a home we've personally verified, a car waiting, and your days planned. Booked from anywhere, in your currency, guaranteed."**

High-level concept: *Concierge-grade Rwanda, one Trip.*

### ④ Solution
- Concierge-verified accommodation (photographed, inspected, guaranteed).
- One **Trip** groups stay, car, and tour, composable, intent-routed, never forced.
- Escrow-backed payments; multi-currency in, local payout out.
- In-app messaging and notifications connecting guests, supply, and the concierge team.

### ⑤ Channels
Diaspora community orgs and churches abroad · diaspora WhatsApp / Facebook groups · Rwanda embassies and events · referral loops · VISIT Rwanda and tour-operator partnerships · targeted social · SEO ("apartment Kigali").

### ⑥ Revenue Streams
Booking commission / margin (headline) · property-management % (supply flywheel) · car margin · tour margin · bundle markup · FX spread.

### ⑦ Cost Structure
Concierge ops team (verification, photography, fulfillment) · payment and FX fees · platform build and hosting · partner payouts · CAC (diaspora marketing).

### ⑧ Key Metrics
Verified listings live · Trips booked · bundle attach rate (car / tour per stay) · GMV · take rate · repeat / referral rate · diaspora to property-management conversion.

### ⑨ Unfair Advantage
Personally-verified, **guaranteed inventory** (a trust product Airbnb can't replicate in Kigali), plus **diaspora property owners as both customers and supply**, plus local ops presence and segment-native UX (currency, rail, language, flow).
