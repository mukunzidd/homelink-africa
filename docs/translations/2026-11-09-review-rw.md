## Still needs a native reader

I am not a native speaker. Everything below was left as it was because I could not judge it with confidence.

Whole areas:
- Smart-search example sentences and placeholders (`smart.example*`, `smart.placeholder*`, `list.requestPlaceholder.*`). Left alone partly because the search parser may read them. Some are stiff ("Minibisi ifite umushoferi y'abantu 10", "Inzu ifite ubusitani abantu 6").
- Help articles and legal pages (`help.articles.*`, `legal.*`). I fixed terminology only. The sentence rhythm of the longer bodies needs a native pass, and `legal.*` needs a lawyer who reads Kinyarwanda.
- Workspace and staff screens (`workspace.*`): housekeeping wording ("Isukura hagati y'abashyitsi", "Isuku hagati mu kuba"), the `workspace.partner.*` use of "icyifuzo" for a job offer, `workspace.admin.percent` ("% bigabanuka").
- Notification bodies with placeholders where the noun class of `{name}` / `{what}` is unknown (`booking.extras.increase`/`decrease` "{name} kimwe", `account.settings.removeBody`, `owner.title.*`).
- Plurals that the RW file collapses into a single form (`sale.bathrooms`, `smart.closestCount`, `reviews.title`, `reviews.new`, `workspace.rates.ownCount`, `workspace.homes.viewingsCount`, `cart.trayAlso`). Not wrong in Kinyarwanda, but worth a look.

Specific strings:
- `roles.guide` "Umuyobozi" vs `roles.admin` "Umuyobozi mukuru": "umuyobozi" also means leader/official. Other strings say "umuyobozi w'ingendo". Possibly "gayide" is what people say.
- `nav.experiences` / `experiences.title` / `landing.daysTitle` "Ingendo": now distinct from the trips menu ("Ingendo zanjye"), but "Gutembera" may read better for a category of days out.
- `listing.sleeps` "Iryamamo {count}", `checkout.guestsHelp` "haryamamo {max}".
- `amenities.security` "Umutekano aho ngaho" (maybe "Umuzamu").
- `cars.class.saloon` "Iy'umujyi", `cars.transmission.manual` "Iy'amaboko" vs `automatic` "Iyikora yonyine" vs `carHighlight.automatic` "Otomatike".
- `results.sort.recommended` "Ibyifuzwa".
- `calendar.done` / `searchPanel.apply` "Byarangiye" as a button.
- `trust.listingsVerified` "Amacumbi {count}" (listings include cars and days out).
- `checkout.noEscrowBody` "Ubwizerane ku byasobanuwe buracyariho".
- `booking.bank.reference` "Indango" and its use in `booking.record.pendingBody`: is "indango" the word on a bank transfer form?
- `abroad.poaBody` "Ububasha bwemejwe na noteri" for power of attorney.
- `help.articles.car-insurance.body` "ubwishingizi bw'undi muntu" for third-party insurance.
- `work.whatPlaceholder` "Douche itemba buhoro" for a slow drain.
- `map.areaLabel` "Agace kegereye i {place}".
- `carHighlight.fullDayBody`, `experienceHighlight.smallGroupBody`: literal calques that still make sense.
- Kirikiri for a car jack, "ipine ry'inyongera" for spare tyre, "4x4", "Yakorewe serivisi" for serviced: I believe these are the spoken forms in Kigali, but please confirm.
- Money agreement with `{amount}`: I used class 6 (ari, yasubijwe, abitswe), following `notifications.kinds.payoutSent.body`. A reader may prefer to avoid the agreement entirely.
- `confirmation.title` "Ibyo wafashe byemejwe": correct but longer than the EN "You're booked". Check it fits.
