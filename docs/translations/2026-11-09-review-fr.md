## Still needs a native reader

- `smart.chipNear` ("{place}, côté {landmark}"): the `smart.landmark.*` values carry no article, so "près de" cannot be built. "côté stade" is natural spoken French but slightly casual for a chip. The other way to fix it is to give `smart.landmark.*` articles and write "près de {landmark}", but "près de le stade" would then break.
- Kigali Convention Centre as "centre de congrès" (`smart.example2`, `landmarks.conventionCentre`, `landmarkNames.conventionCentre`, `places.Kimihurura`). That is standard French, but Kigali residents often just say "Convention Center" or "KCC". A Rwandan reader should pick.
- "Excursion" for a day out across the whole file (it was split between "sortie" and "excursion"). It is the usual tourism word and avoids "sortie" also meaning "exit", but it is three letters longer in tabs and chips (`smart.lane.experiences`, `queue.lane.experience`, `saved.kind.experience`). Check the fit in the browser.
- "Fermer à la réservation" for "take off sale" in maintenance (`workspace.work.block*`, `offSale`). The literal "retirer de la vente" read as if the house were up for sale. Ops staff may already use a word of their own for this, such as "bloquer".
- `list.ctaApply` is now "Demander à louer" (16 characters, the English is "Apply"). Check that it fits on the list row button.
- `owner.label` ("Votre annonce, côté prestataire"): the English "Your side of this listing" has no clean French equivalent.
- `workspace.finance.escrowNote` ("versé automatiquement pour le logement"): the English says the money is "released to the home". If it actually goes to the owner, write "au propriétaire".
- `booking.extras.increase` / `decrease` ("Augmenter : {name}") are accessible labels written to avoid the unknown gender of {name}. They read correctly, but mechanically.
- `account.settings.expires` ("Expire {date}") was left as is because the date format is unknown. If it renders as MM/AA, "Expire fin {date}" would read better.
- Apostrophes: the file mixes straight (about 435) and curly (about 62, mostly in strings added later). I left this alone, because a straight apostrophe in front of `{` changes ICU parsing and a bulk swap carries more risk than benefit. New text follows the apostrophe style of the string it replaces.
- Guest vs client: public pages say "voyageur", the staff workspace says "client". Both are natural for their audience, so I kept the split on purpose.
