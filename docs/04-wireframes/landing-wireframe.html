How to read this

1Context adapts to the visitor.Language, currency and rail default from location and are always overridable via “change”.
2Duration sets the flow.Short / medium means nightly booking; long term switches to the leasing flow before results load.
3Intent doors, not a gate.They route by why you came, but sit on top of a feed you can scroll straight past. The most relevant door is highlighted per segment.
4Feed is always browsable.Locals who just want to scroll see listings immediately, framed for their segment.
5Bundle is gravity.“Add a car / tour” hints (amber) pull toward a Trip for visitors, and never appear on residential listings.
Segment: visitor from abroad. Diaspora and tourist framing, card-first, Trip prompts on.
